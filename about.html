FOUNDER & CEO

Khushboo Agrawal

Khushboo Agrawal studied Computer Science and Business, Technology & Entrepreneurship at New York University, where her exposure to New York's startup ecosystem shaped much of her thinking about innovation.

What stood out wasn't just the ideas being built, but the ecosystem behind them: access to mentors, technology, resources, and people willing to help solve difficult problems. She saw how much of a difference the right environment could make, and began questioning why that kind of support wasn't more widely accessible.

That thinking led to Quantedge. With a hands-on approach to problem-solving, an appetite for ambitious ideas, and little patience for accepting things simply because they've always been done a certain way, Khushboo is building Quantedge to bring technology, talent, and opportunity closer together.
