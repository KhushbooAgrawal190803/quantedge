WHO IT'S FOR

Built for institutions.
Felt by every learner.

We train whole cohorts, not single seats. Programs are shaped around the people in the room and the work they will go back to, taught live in English, with Hindi where it helps. Based in Ranchi, working with institutions across India.

01 / COLLEGES

Colleges & universities

Applied AI for students in any discipline, so they graduate able to use the tools their first employer already does.

02 / VOCATIONAL

Diploma & vocational institutes

Practical AI for learners heading into trades, offices and shop floors, with no programming background assumed.

03 / SCHOOLS

Schools & teachers

AI literacy for senior students and hands-on sessions that help teachers plan, prepare and assess with confidence.

04 / TEAMS

Companies & teams

Workshops and longer programs for staff who want to use AI on the reports, sheets and email they handle every day.

INSIDE A CLASS

One class.
Three moves.

A vague ask becomes a clear one.
